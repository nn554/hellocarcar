# HelloCarCar Product Spec

## Positioning

An Algeria-first procurement decision assistant for Arabic-speaking buyers sourcing Chinese vehicles. The product reduces information asymmetry before purchase and keeps PI/VIN logistics visible after purchase.

## MVP

- Arabic RTL, French, English customer web/PWA; Chinese operations view.
- Vehicle catalog with brand, energy, body, seats and price filters.
- FOB price ranges with source date, incoterm and verification state.
- Masked supplier preview; verified paid reports can reveal the full report.
- Quote + manual T/T proof review; payment provider remains replaceable.
- PI/VIN ownership-checked logistics lookup.

## Explicit non-goals

No vehicle cart, inventory reservation, online vehicle sale, or supplier self-service onboarding in MVP.

## Trust rules

Facts shown to customers must have an evidence record, source date and review state. Low-confidence AI answers become human-review tasks.
