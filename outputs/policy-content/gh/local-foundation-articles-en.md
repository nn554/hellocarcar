# Ghana vehicle import basics: practical guides for buyers and importers

Status: `DRAFT` | Language: English | Market: Ghana | Updated: 2 September 2026

These articles use a local news-style structure: the answer first, a short explanation, numbered steps, and a clear warning. They are general information, not customs, tax or legal advice.

## 1. Can you import this vehicle into Ghana? Check the importer, vehicle and port before paying

### The short answer

Most vehicle imports must be declared to Ghana Customs through the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS) and cleared by an importer or licensed customs house. A foreign seller's invoice does not replace the Ghana-side importer, tax and registration requirements.

Ghana does not use one simple “all vehicles under X years” rule for every transaction. Vehicle age, condition, right-hand/left-hand drive, salvage history, safety and registration requirements can affect whether the vehicle is accepted and at what cost. Confirm the current rule for the exact VIN before shipment.

### Steps before shipment

1. Confirm the Ghana importer has a Taxpayer Identification Number/Ghana Card details and an authorised customs agent.
2. Check the VIN, first registration date, engine or motor type, country of origin and vehicle condition.
3. Ask the agent to confirm the HS classification and whether any current restriction, standards check or port instruction applies.
4. Agree who will pay customs duty, VAT, port handling, storage, inspection and inland delivery.
5. Keep the written clearance and registration plan with the sales contract.

### Main risks

Do not rely on a seller's statement that a vehicle is “duty free” or “easy to register”. Customs valuation and Ghana-side taxes are determined from the declaration and supporting documents. A vehicle that arrives with an incorrect description or damaged/salvage history may face delay, extra examination or refusal.

Official starting points: [Ghana Revenue Authority Customs](https://gra.gov.gh/customs/) and [GRA vehicle importation guidance](https://gra.gov.gh/customs/vehicle-importation/). Confirm the live ICUMS and DVLA requirements with a licensed Ghana customs house.

## 2. Ghana vehicle clearance: the documents to prepare before the ship arrives

### The short answer

The bill of lading, commercial invoice, export or foreign registration documents and the vehicle's identification details should match exactly. The Ghana agent uses these records to lodge the customs declaration in ICUMS and arrange examination, payment and release.

### A practical document checklist

1. Bill of lading or sea waybill showing the consignee and vehicle details.
2. Commercial invoice or sale contract with price, currency, seller and buyer.
3. Foreign registration/title or deregistration/export certificate, where applicable.
4. Packing list or vehicle details sheet, including VIN, make, model, year and engine/motor data.
5. Importer identity, Ghana tax details and the appointment of a licensed customs agent.
6. Any inspection, standards, insurance or port documents requested for this vehicle category.

### What happens at the port

The agent lodges the declaration, Customs checks the classification and value, and the vehicle may be examined. After assessed charges are paid and no hold remains, the release process can proceed. Registration with the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Authority (DVLA) is a separate step from customs release.

### Warning

Names, VINs, currencies and dates must be consistent across the invoice, bill of lading and declaration. Ask for a copy of the submitted declaration and an itemised statement before making additional payments.

Official starting points: [GRA Customs](https://gra.gov.gh/customs/), [Ghana Ports and Harbours Authority](https://www.ghanaports.gov.gh/) and [DVLA Ghana](https://www.dvla.gov.gh/). Port charges and document lists can change; obtain the current checklist from the appointed agent.

## 3. Ghana import cost: do not calculate the final price from one percentage

### The short answer

The landed cost normally combines the customs-assessed value, import duty and other statutory charges, VAT or related taxes, plus port, examination, storage, agency and inland transport fees. The exact amount depends on the HS code, customs value, vehicle type, origin and rules in force on the declaration date.

### How to build a usable estimate

1. Record the vehicle price, freight, insurance and any additions used for the customs value.
2. Confirm the HS code and valuation method with a licensed Ghana customs agent.
3. Request a written breakdown separating duty, taxes, levies and port or private-service charges.
4. Check whether a standards inspection, environmental charge, storage period or examination fee applies.
5. Reconfirm the estimate shortly before shipment and again when the declaration is prepared.

### What not to publish

Do not advertise a universal “Ghana car import tax rate”. A rate quoted for one engine, origin or declaration cannot be transferred to another vehicle. The official tariff and the agent's current assessment should control the customer quote.

Official starting points: [GRA Customs](https://gra.gov.gh/customs/) and [GRA tax information](https://gra.gov.gh/). This article does not promise a duty amount or an exemption.
